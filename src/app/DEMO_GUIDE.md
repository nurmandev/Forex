## 📝 4-Step Subscription Process

### **Step 1: Plan Details & Personal Information**
- View plan summary with:
  - Investment amount
  - Daily profit percentage
  - Min duration and withdrawal limits
- Enter personal information:
  - Full Name
  - Email Address
- Security notice displayed
- Click "Continue to Payment"

### **Step 2: Payment Method Selection**
Choose from 3 payment options:
- 💳 **Credit/Debit Card** (Visa, Mastercard, Amex)
- 🏦 **Bank Transfer** (Direct bank transfer)
- ₿ **Cryptocurrency** (Bitcoin, Ethereum, USDT)

*Note: Payment is simulated for demo purposes*

Click "Continue"

### **Step 3: MT4/MT5 Credentials**
This is the key step where trading account is linked:

**Important Alert Shown:**
- Explains read-only access needed
- Military-grade encryption mentioned
- Admin review process explained

**Form Fields:**
1. **Account Type:** Choose MT4 or MT5 (radio buttons)
2. **Account Username/Login:** Your MT4/MT5 login ID
3. **Server Name:** Broker server (e.g., ICMarkets-Demo01)
4. **Account Password:** MT4/MT5 password
5. **Confirm Password:** Re-enter password

**Validation:**
- All fields are required
- Passwords must match
- Helper text provided for each field

Click "Review Order"

### **Step 4: Confirmation & Review**
Review all details:

**Plan Details:**
- Plan name, investment amount, daily profit, min duration

**Personal Information:**
- Name and email

**Payment Method:**
- Selected payment option

**Trading Account:**
- Account type, username, server

**Terms & Conditions:**
- Checkbox with 4 key points:
  - Trading risks acknowledged
  - Credentials securely stored
  - Pending status until admin approval
  - Performance disclaimers

Click "Complete Subscription"

## 🔄 Subscription Status States

### 1. **Pending** (Yellow)
- Awaiting admin review
- Shows submission time
- Can't withdraw or trade yet
- Full details visible

### 2. **Active** (Green)
- Admin approved
- Shows approval time
- Trading enabled
- Profits being tracked
- Withdrawals available

### 3. **Rejected** (Red)
- Credentials invalid or issues found
- Support contact suggested
- Can resubmit with corrections

### 4. **Expired** (Gray)
- Subscription period ended
- Can renew or upgrade
